5. Cookies and browser storage

The website does not use analytics or marketing cookies and does not track visitors. We only use browser storage (localStorage) to remember your list of favourite products and that the cookie notice was closed. This data stays on your device only – we do not send it to ourselves. You can delete it in your browser settings.

The website's fonts are stored on our server, so loading them does not send data to Google. Links to the dladomu.sklep.pl shop, Amazon, Allegro and social media lead to external sites that have their own privacy policies.
