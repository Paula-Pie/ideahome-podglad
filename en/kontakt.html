Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy IDEA HOME products?

Our products are available online on Amazon.de. In Poland you'll also find them at dladomu.sklep.pl and at our trade partners.

What's the difference between strong and extra-strong bags?

Extra-strong bags have thicker film. They are ideal for garden debris, branches and heavy waste. Strong bags are enough for everyday household waste.

Which bag colours match which waste fractions?

Yellow – metals and plastics, blue – paper, green – glass, brown – organic, black – mixed waste (Polish system).

Can Flexistore boxes be bought individually?

We currently offer them in sets of 4 sizes, in Denim and Neutral. Ask us if you need more of one size.
